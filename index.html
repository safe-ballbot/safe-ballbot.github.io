Safe-Ballbot: Tilt-Consistent MPC with Control Barrier Functions for Ballbot Navigation

TL;DR

A ballbot must lean to accelerate. We plan on a simple jerk model with control barrier functions, bound its acceleration by the steepest lean that a tilt-safe tracker (HSMC-ESO-ECBF) allows, and inflate predicted obstacles with conformal prediction. The result avoids static, moving, rotating, concave and unpredictable obstacles in MuJoCo, plans in about 10 ms, and never asks the robot to lean more than it safely can.
